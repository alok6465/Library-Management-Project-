Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

Information We Collect

LibraryPro collects and processes the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, PRN number, email address, phone number, and address
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted passwords and login credentials
  • Library Activity: Book borrowing history, loan records, and fine information
  • System Data: Login timestamps and system usage statistics

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Library service provision and book loan management
  • Communication regarding library services and overdue books
  • System administration and security monitoring
  • Statistical analysis for service improvement

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Password encryption using industry-standard hashing algorithms
  • Secure session management with CSRF protection
  • Role-based access control to limit data access
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users and system
  • With your explicit consent

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information stored in the system
  • Update or correct your profile information
  • Request deletion of your account (subject to library policies)
  • Receive information about data processing activities
